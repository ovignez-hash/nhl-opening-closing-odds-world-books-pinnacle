### How to read it

**Prices are decimal odds** (European format), up to 4 decimals: `1.91`, `2.375`. A stake of 1 returns the price, stake included.

**Moneyline includes overtime and the shootout.** It is the two-way home/away market.

**1X2 is regular time only:** home win, draw, away win after 60 minutes. A game tied after 60 minutes is a draw, whatever happens in overtime; `Result_60` settles it.

**The puck line is ±1.5 only, and it is the home team's line.** `-1.5` means the home team gives 1.5 goals; the away team's line is the opposite. A ±1.5 puck line settles the same way with or without overtime, since a game decided in overtime or a shootout is won by exactly one goal. Each book's puck line is its standard one, with the favourite at -1.5: of the two ±1.5 pairs, the more balanced one.

**Two kinds of totals, never mixed in one column.** `Total` counts overtime and the shootout (the winner's shootout goal counts, as in the final score): Pinnacle, Bet365, Betway. `RegTotal` counts regular time only, 60 minutes: Bwin, SBOBET, 1xBet. The two settle differently on most lines: tied 3-3 after 60 minutes, a game is under 6.5 in regular time and over 6.5 once overtime or the shootout adds a goal. Each book's definition was established game by game, comparing its prices with other books on the same line in the same games; Bwin's totals before 2011-12 and after 2019-20, SBOBET's before 2014-15, and Betfair Exchange's, whose definition could not be established, are left out. On the whole file, each column's prices match the outcome of its own definition. Closing prices on the 4.5, 6.5 and 8.5 lines, where overtime changes the result:

**The total line is in goals**, the same for over and under; quarter lines (5.25, 5.75) are split stakes.

**One line per book, market and phase: the main line.** At the open, it is the first line the book offered: among the lines it posted in its first two hours, the most balanced one (two prices closest to each other). When a total line was clearly not the main one, with its two prices more than 0.50 apart, no opening price is given (1,725 cases). At the close, it is the most balanced of the lines the book updated in its last hour of updates, provided its two prices are at most 0.15 apart; otherwise the most balanced of all its lines.

**One time per book, market and phase.** The `_Time` columns are ISO 8601 in UTC and give the moment the price was recorded. Most prices are stamped to the minute (seconds `00`); Pinnacle's, Bet365's puck line and total from 2018, and 1xBet's 2026-27 moneyline are stamped to the second.

**The opening price is the first price recorded for the main line.** Median age of the opening moneyline at the scheduled start: Pinnacle 29 hours, Bet365 24 hours, William Hill 27 hours, Bwin 28 hours, Betway 24 hours, Betfair Exchange 24 hours, SBOBET 24 hours, 1xBet 64 hours. No opening is older than 14 days.

**Openings older than 14 days were left out** (1,753 prices, 395 games): a first price recorded more than 14 days before the start belongs to the original date of a postponed game, or to a line posted long ahead.

**Placeholder and reversed openings were left out:** an opening moneyline with the same price on both teams while the other books opened more than 0.10 away from a toss-up in no-vig probability, or, when no other book opened, while the closing market is more than 0.15 away (11 prices); and an opening moneyline whose two prices were reversed: swapped, they match the median of the other books' openings within 0.05, while as published they are more than 0.15 away from it and from the close (23 prices).

**The closing price is the last price the book recorded before the start.** `Kickoff_UTC` is the scheduled start; the puck drops a few minutes later and Pinnacle takes bets until then (median closing time 5 min after the scheduled start). A price is recorded when it changes, so a line the book did not move in its last hours carries an older time. Pinnacle's prices and Bet365's puck line and total from 2018 are recorded at every change: their closing price is recorded between 12 hours before and 30 minutes after the scheduled start. For the other prices, a closing price unchanged for up to 36 hours is kept; nothing older. Median closing time against the scheduled start:

**Closing prices were checked against the market.** For each game, the reference is the median of the closing values of all the other books recorded for that game, including books that are not in this file: the no-vig home probability on the moneyline (each side's 1/price divided by the sum of both), the home team's no-vig probability on the same puck line, and the total line. A closing price was left out when it was more than 0.05 away in no-vig probability on the moneyline, more than 0.10 on the puck line, or 1 goal or more away on the total (170 prices). With fewer than three other books recorded, a total line at least 1 goal from every other recorded book's line was left out (7 prices). Opening prices more than 0.30 away in no-vig probability on the moneyline or the puck line, or 2 goals on the total, from that same reference were left out (2 prices). A 1X2 price whose home win plus half the draw is more than 0.08 (close) or 0.20 (open) away from the moneyline market was left out (22 prices). A puck line giving -1.5 to the team the same book's moneyline (or Pinnacle's), recorded within 3 hours of it, makes the underdog, at a no-vig probability below 0.42, was left out (72 prices).

**Prices that cannot exist were not used:** prices below 1.01; two-way markets with a margin below 1% or above 12% (Betfair Exchange: below 0% or above 6%); 1X2 margins outside 1-20% (Betfair Exchange: 0-8%); total prices outside 1.40-3.50; puck line prices outside 1.05-6.00; total lines outside 3.5-9.5 goals and lines that are not a multiple of 0.25; opening prices recorded after the closing price or after the start. The margin is checked on the main line once it is chosen: when it fails, the book has no price for that market and phase. When the opening record is the closing record itself (same moment), it appears once, as the closing price.

## Said plainly

- **2006-07 and 2007-08 carry the 1X2 only** (regular time): no moneyline, puck line or total was recorded for those seasons. The moneyline starts in 2008-09.
- **232 games have no price at all**, 174 of them among the 185 games played from 1 to 28 November 2007, and most of the others in November and December 2006. They stay in the file so that the schedule is complete.
- **Pinnacle starts on 1 August 2020**, with the 2020 playoffs. No Pinnacle price exists for 2006-07 to 2019-20's regular season. From August 2020, 469 games have no Pinnacle moneyline: all 311 Arizona Coyotes games from August 2020 to 2023-24, all 82 Utah Hockey Club games of 2024-25, 61 games of the 2020 playoffs and 15 others. On these games Pinnacle has a total on 309 and a puck line on 145, mostly opening prices (48 closing totals, 19 closing puck lines), and 145 of these games have no Pinnacle price at all. 432 Pinnacle totals and puck lines come from a second timestamped record (totals from February 2021, puck lines from 2023-24); 390 of them have an opening price only.
- **Bet365's puck line and total** cover 2008-09 and 2009-10 (then a handful of games in 2010-11 and 2011-12), part of 2012-13 (puck line on 21% of games, total on 8%), then every season from 31 December 2017. In 2026-27 so far, its closing puck line is on 203 games and its closing total on 2.
- **William Hill carries the moneyline from 17 February 2009 and the 1X2 from 6 October 2006.** It is absent from the start of 2025-26 until 31 March 2026, and back from 1 April 2026.
- **Bwin's total is regular time**, from 6 October 2011 to 11 March 2020. Its moneyline is missing from 30 January 2014 to 7 October 2014.
- **Betway prices the 1X2 from 29 September 2007 and the moneyline from 29 January 2009** (missing from 23 March 2015 to 11 October 2016). Its puck line covers 2008-09 to 2011-12 (then one game in 2012-13) and is back from 15 November 2024; its total covers 2008-09 and 2009-10 (then 6% of 2010-11) and is back from 14 December 2024.
- **Betfair Exchange carries the moneyline (from 11 February 2009, missing from 12 June 2014 to 29 February 2016) and the 1X2 from 1 December 2007 to 11 June 2014.** Its prices appear to be net of the exchange's commission on winnings: the median margin at the close is 3.3%, above Pinnacle's 2.5%. An exchange market usually opens before any money is matched, so most of its openings had no real price and were left out.
- **SBOBET carries the moneyline from 19 March 2009 to 13 June 2014** (then on 5 games) **and its regular-time total from 8 October 2014 to 16 February 2022.** It has no puck line: its main handicaps are Asian lines around 0 to 1 goal.
- **1xBet prices the 1X2, the puck line and its regular-time total from October 2014, and the moneyline from 20 January 2021** (none in 2025-26).
- **2 games were listed with home and away reversed** in the recorded odds; they are given in the official orientation, with the prices and puck lines swapped accordingly.
- **Start times are the NHL's scheduled times.** In 2006-07 and 2007-08, the start times listed with the odds were often wrong by several hours; the official times are used throughout.
- **The 2026-27 season stops on 31 October 2026**, 226 games. Later months come with the monthly update.
